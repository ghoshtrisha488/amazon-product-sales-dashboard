# 📊 Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence dashboard built in Power BI to analyze e-commerce retail performance, revenue drivers, sales velocity, and customer review metrics[cite: 1].

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project simulates real-world retail analytics for Amazon product sales, transforming raw transactional data into actionable executive insights[cite: 1]. It features dynamic time-intelligence filtering, category-level revenue distribution, and identification of top-performing products to optimize inventory and marketing strategies[cite: 1].

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features & Metrics
* **Executive KPIs:** Real-time tracking of YTD Sales ($2.18M), QTD Sales ($811.09K), Total Units Sold (27.75K), and Customer Reviews (19.42M)[cite: 1].
* **Trend Analysis:** Visual representation of monthly and weekly sales dynamics to pinpoint peak purchasing windows[cite: 1].
* **Category Breakdown:** Granular revenue tracking across verticals including Men's Shoes, Camera Equipment, Car Accessories, Toys, and Mobile Accessories[cite: 1].
* **Top Performers Insights:** Ranked top 5 products by YTD sales revenue and total review volume[cite: 1].
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering capabilities by product categories, quarters, and months for deep-dive exploration[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Data Modeling & Analytics:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Time-Intelligence calculations, and cross-filtering data architecture[cite: 1].
* **UI/UX Design:** Executive dark-themed dashboard layout optimized for readability, tooltips, and seamless user interaction[cite: 1].
